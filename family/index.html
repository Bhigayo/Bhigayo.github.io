How to

Get your chat out of WhatsApp

iPhone

  1. Open the group chat and tap the group name.
  2. Scroll down, tap Export chat, then Without media.
  3. Choose Save to Files.
  4. Come back here, tap Open chat export and pick the .zip.

Android

  1. Open the group chat and tap the three dots.
  2. Tap More, then Export chat, then Without media.
  3. Save it to your phone or Drive.
  4. Come back here and open the .txt file.

The roles

  1. Everyone gets exactly one role.
  2. Roles go to whoever earns them most, from the Don down.
  3. Tap Add mugshot on any file to pin a photo.
